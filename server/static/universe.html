The machine

Seven stages. No neural network. No LLM.

Rules, timelines, dynamic programming and two small models trained on the 1,900 labelled practice rows. Everything runs offline: 20,000 sentences in about three and a half minutes, on two CPUs.

04 Truth engine

Truth has a timeline.

Every other page that mentions Jules's languages lands on one timeline. We fit the most likely staircase through them: a lone wrong value is outvoted, a real change, repeated by later pages, becomes a new step.

Pages earn trust as they go: a rumour page counts less than a release note, and the timelines are refit.

05 Judge

Weigh the evidence.

Does the value sit on its staircase on its date? Was it true before (stale), only later (premature), or never (invented)? How many pages agree? Does a confirmed row nearby disagree?

A forest of decision trees weighs it all. It learned from the labelled rows without ever seeing a row's own label, exactly as it will judge a new one.
